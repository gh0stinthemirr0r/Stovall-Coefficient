# Limitations and research roadmap

## Current limitations

1. No assembled local joint distribution of age, gender identity, orientation, partnership, dating intent and bilateral consideration.
2. ACS sex and marital-status categories are not comprehensive gender identity or dating availability measures.
3. Published survey percentages are not conditional probabilities of a particular pair's mutual interest.
4. National surveys may not transport to San Diego without bias.
5. Real-world encounter feasibility depends on time horizon, transport, accessibility, work and social networks.
6. Small subgroup sample sizes create instability and disclosure risk.
7. A single numerical SC can conceal profound structural uncertainty; ranges and component estimates are essential.

## Development phases

**Phase 0 — Definition:** freeze estimands, identity schema, ethics policy, geographic boundaries and reporting standard. **Phase 1 — Population:** acquire ACS cells and margins of error; build reproducible data pipeline. **Phase 2 — Evidence:** assess survey suitability and coverage; produce partially identified bounds. **Phase 3 — Estimation:** fit joint or conditional models with proper uncertainty and sensitivity analysis. **Phase 4 — Validation:** prospectively measure reciprocal consideration, exposure and calibration in an independent sample. **Phase 5 — Publication:** independent methodological review, versioned archive, replication materials and limitations disclosure.

## Publication claims policy

Acceptable: "We propose a framework and provide synthetic arithmetic verification." Not acceptable without new evidence: "The coefficient accurately predicts San Diego dating availability" or "X residents would date a specific person." Do not imply peer review, original empirical findings, or validated precision.
