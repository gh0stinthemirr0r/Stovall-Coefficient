# Research questions and testable hypotheses

**RQ1 — Identification.** What components of an aggregate reciprocal dating-opportunity pool are identifiable from available local demographic tables and representative dating surveys? Hypothesis: age-eligible counts are substantially better identified than reciprocal preference probabilities.

**RQ2 — Reciprocal age acceptance.** Do age-specific bilateral acceptance functions outperform a symmetric fixed-age-band rule in held-out predictive calibration? Hypothesis: a properly calibrated bilateral model improves calibration; this remains untested.

**RQ3 — Geographic transport.** How much do national dating survey estimates miscalibrate when transported to San Diego County? Compare weighted predictions against a locally recruited validation sample.

**RQ4 — Identity coverage.** How do missing sexual-orientation and gender-identity measures affect aggregate estimates and interval width? Hypothesis: honest treatment of missingness widens uncertainty, especially for small subgroups.

**RQ5 — Encounter feasibility.** Does modeling contact opportunities improve prediction of reciprocal consideration realized within a defined horizon compared with demographic eligibility alone?

**RQ6 — Robustness.** How sensitive is SC to relationship-intent categories, age filtering, survey question wording, population vintage, and the definition of availability?
