# Frequently asked questions

**Is this the original Wolowitz coefficient?** No. That fictional expression is the cultural inspiration; this is a distinct methodological proposal.

**Can it estimate men, women, transgender and nonbinary populations?** Conceptually yes, when distinct persons are counted once and compatible, representative data are available. Current data do not support every local intersection.

**Does the model assume heterosexuality?** No. Reference identity, candidate identity and expressed partner preferences are separate, optional attributes.

**Can older people be included?** Yes. Age 42 with candidates aged 25–58 includes older and younger adults. There is no age-based exclusion except adulthood and optional user filters.

**Can it predict who will date me?** No. An aggregate probability does not identify willing individuals or imply consent.

**Is it validated?** No. This repository contains a proposed estimand, research protocols and a synthetic calculation demonstration.

**Why not multiply published percentages?** They may refer to different populations and correlated events. A joint or conditional model is required.

**Why not report a precise San Diego number now?** Sufficient age-specific, gender-inclusive, reciprocal preference data have not been assembled or validated. Precision without evidence is misleading.

**Does transgender status determine orientation?** No. They are distinct constructs.

**What is the unit of output?** Expected number of unique potentially receptive adults in a defined population and time horizon; not dates, matches, or sexual partners.
