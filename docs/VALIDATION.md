# Validation and falsification protocol

## Purpose

Test whether the model predicts a **defined aggregate reciprocal-consideration event** in a prospectively sampled adult population. Do not validate against self-reported attractiveness, an individual's worth, or presumed consent.

## Study design

Recruit a voluntary sample of adults with coverage beyond dating apps. Use privacy-preserving surveys of relationship availability, relationship intent, age consideration, partner-gender preferences and encounter channels. Obtain informed consent and permit nonresponse. Record survey design, inclusion probabilities, nonresponse and attrition. Reserve independent temporal and geographic holdouts.

## Outcomes

Define separately (1) reciprocal willingness to consider, (2) feasible opportunity for contact within a prespecified horizon, and (3) opt-in mutual expressions of interest where ethically observable. Do not substitute matches for dates, or dates for relationships. The latter outcomes require additional models.

## Metrics

- Aggregate observed-to-expected ratios by preregistered cells.
- Calibration plots and weighted Brier scores for individual probability predictions when individual outcomes are available.
- Interval coverage, effective sample size and uncertainty calibration.
- Geographic and temporal transport degradation.
- Selection sensitivity, including platform-only versus broader sampling frames.
- Subgroup calibration only with sufficient effective sample sizes and disclosure-safe reporting.

## Falsification tests

A coherent model must preserve D >= R >= O and must not assign zero to older adults simply because they are older. Predictions should degrade or widen when preference information is withheld, rather than silently becoming certain. Compare against naive age-only and demographic-only baselines. Require a documented out-of-sample improvement before claiming predictive value.

## Success criteria

Preregister numerical thresholds based on a power analysis once a sampling frame and endpoint are fixed. Do not fabricate thresholds or claim validation before data collection. Independent replication and peer review are necessary next steps.
