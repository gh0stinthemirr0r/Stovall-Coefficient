# Glossary

**Adult:** person aged 18 or older for the research scope. **Candidate:** distinct person potentially included in a population cell. **Reference person:** adult whose profile anchors the calculation. **Reciprocal consideration:** both parties would consider each other in a defined hypothetical or observed context, not consent to contact. **Availability:** self-described openness to relevant dating arrangements, not simply unmarried status. **Relationship intent:** long-term, casual, either or other expressed goal. **Orientation:** self-described sexual/romantic orientation, not a deterministic preference matrix. **Gender identity:** self-described gender; distinct from transgender experience. **Joint probability:** probability of all required events together. **Conditional probability:** probability of an event given specified other information/events. **Transportability:** whether source-population relationships apply to a target population. **Partial identification:** bounding an estimand when evidence cannot identify a unique value. **Encounter feasibility:** probability of realistic contact opportunity within a defined horizon. **Calibration:** agreement between predicted probabilities and observed frequencies. **Disclosure control:** measures to prevent revealing information about identifiable individuals. **Synthetic data:** fabricated values used for demonstration and tests, never evidence of real prevalence.
